# Story

## Premise

"Death by Midnight" is a time loop mystery where you play as a detective who requires everyone in the manor to survive by midnight for the will to take effect.

The owner has passed away and hired you to carry out his will. You arrive at the manor and need to wait until the next day to see the full will. But someone at the manor dies, then you restart back in time at 6pm. Your goal is to investigate the manor residents and the cause of the deaths in the previous loop.
