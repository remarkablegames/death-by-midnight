# Story

## Premise

"Death by Midnight" is a time-loop mystery where you play as a detective who seeks to uncover the truth and prevent the residents' deaths.

The Master of the manor has passed away, and you're hired to execute his will by the next day. The will cannot be read until midnight. When someone at the manor dies before the reading, the night snaps back to 6 p.m. and begins again and you are the only one who remembers.

## Loop Rules

- The night runs from 6 p.m. to midnight; the will is read at midnight.
- If anyone dies before the reading, time resets to 6 p.m. and the night replays.
- Only the detective remembers previous loops. The household, their moods, and the placement of evidence all reset.
- Persistent knowledge takes the form of unlocked talk options: what the detective learns endures as what he can now ask about.
- Physical items reset each loop and must be refound. A few (the basement key) are handed over again through the persistent conversations.

## Mechanics

- **Time progression**: the clock runs from 6 p.m. to midnight.
- **Exploration**: roam the manor and grounds to find documents, objects, and secret rooms.
- **Conversation**: talk to characters to uncover their secrets; knowledge carries across loops as new talk options.
- **Inventory**: pick up items, read them, and give them to characters to change their behaviour.
- **No save/load mid-night**: the loop itself is the reset. Persistent variables track what the detective knows.

## Themes

- Family
- Secrets
- Time
- Inheritance (what a legacy does to the people who inherit it)

## Plot

The important rule for a time-loop story is that each loop should add knowledge, not merely repeat events.

| Loop | What happens | What the detective learns |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Madelyn is found dead. The detective has no foreknowledge and cannot prevent it. | The death itself; the shape of the night. |
| 2 | The detective investigates Madelyn's final movements. Evidence points to Ben, but before he can be exposed, Nora poisons him to keep him from declaring Mia illegitimate at the reading. | The affair; the poison; the discovery that the will about to be read aloud is a decoy. |
| 3 | "A different death": Mia, having learned part of the truth about her parentage, takes her own life. There is no murderer, and no one benefits. | The pattern breaks. The detective stops asking who kills and starts asking who drives whom to die. |
| Fail-state | An accusation aimed at the wrong person turns Madelyn's rage onto Nora instead. | A misplaced accusation never prevents a death; it redirects one. |
| Final | The detective reconstructs the Master's scheme and confronts the cause of the loop. | Why the detective is trapped; the Master's hand in every death. |

Knowledge milestones to unlock across loops:

- Discover the affair between the Butler and the Maid.
- Learn that Mia is the Master's daughter.
- Learn why the Nurse protects the Master.
- Discover who benefits from each death and why Madelyn's and Mia's benefit no one.
- Learn through dialogue that the Nurse and the Maid are sisters (Madelyn is Nora's younger sister and Mia's aunt); surfaced along Nora's "why I protect the Master" talk track.
- Discover why the detective is trapped in the loop.

## The Cursed Will

The will read aloud at midnight is a decoy. The Master bound his estate to a clause: the night repeats whenever a death occurs until the family's history is confessed and the true heir is named. A scroll left in the rafters hints that "the will you seek is not the one they will read aloud."

- The _True Will_ is hidden in the basement, behind a gate the Master kept locked.
- The Master kept the only key; a spare rests with Nora, and a further spare is hidden in the bedroom.
- Presenting the _True Will_ at the midnight reading in the entrance hall is what ends the loop.

## Endings

- **Good ending**: The detective prevents the night's death by easing the confrontation, but the full truth stays buried and the decoy will stands.
- **Bad ending**: The detective accuses the wrong person or fails to prevent a death before midnight.
- **True ending**: The detective exposes the Master's manipulation, reveals Mia's true parentage, presents the _True Will_ at the reading, and breaks the loop.
- **Hidden ending**: The detective discovers that the Master deliberately created the time loop and instead of saving the family, takes up the Master's pocket watch, inherits the manor, and becomes the new Master, trapped inside the night forever, now as the one who runs it.
