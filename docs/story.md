# Story

## Premise

"Death by Midnight" is a time loop mystery where you play as a detective who seeks to uncover the truth and prevent the residents' deaths.

The owner has passed away and you're hired to execute his will by the next day. When someone at the manor dies, you restart back in time.

## Mechanics

- Time progression
- Talk to characters to uncover their secrets
- Explore and find secret documents
- No save/load but choices and routes can be unlocked via persistent variables

## Themes

- Family
- Secrets
- Time

## Plot

## Plot

### Loop 1: The first death

- The detective arrives at 6 p.m.
- The household prepares for dinner.
- The Owner's will cannot be read until midnight.
- Someone dies before the will is revealed.
- The detective returns to 6 p.m.

### Loop 2: The obvious suspect

- The detective investigates the victim's final movements.
- Evidence points toward the Butler.
- The Butler dies before midnight.

### Loop 3: The family secret

- The detective discovers the affair between the Butler and Maid.
- The Miss learns part of the truth about her parentage.
- A different death occurs.

### Final loops

- The detective reconstructs the Owner's scheme.
- The cause of the time loop is revealed.
- The detective chooses whether to expose, protect, or manipulate the family.

The important rule for a time-loop story is that each loop should add knowledge, not merely repeat events.

- Discover the affair
- Learn that the miss is the owner's daughter
- Learn why the nurse protects the owner
- Discover who benefits from each death
- Discover why the detective is trapped in the loop

## Endings

- Good ending
  - The detective prevents the deaths but does not uncover the complete truth.
- Bad ending
  - The detective accuses the wrong person or fails to prevent the final death.
- True ending
  - The detective exposes the owner's manipulation, reveals the miss's parentage, and breaks the loop.
- Hidden ending
  - The detective discovers that the owner deliberately created the time loop and chooses to inherit the manor rather than save the family.
