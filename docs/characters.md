# Characters

- Detective

- Nurse

## Profiles

### Butler Ben

- **Public role:** Butler, head of household, and Nora's husband
- **Public goal:** Maintain order in the manor and ensure that the family's inheritance remains secure
- **Private goal:** Inherit the manor for himself and gain control over the family's future
- **Secret:** He is having an affair with Madelyn
- **Secret he does not know:** Mia is the Owner's biological daughter, making Ben her uncle rather than her father
- **Fear:** Losing his position, authority, and the family's respect
- **Misbelief:** He believes controlling the family is the same as protecting it
- **Personality:** Ambitious, controlling, confident, pragmatic, and curt
- **Relationship with Nora:** Married to her, but increasingly distant and resentful
- **Relationship with Madelyn:** Romantically involved with her, though he may see the relationship as temporary
- **Relationship with Mia:** Believes she is his daughter and wants her loyalty
- **Arc:** From trying to control the family and secure the inheritance to confronting the consequences of his lies

### Maid Madelyn

- **Public role:** Maid at the manor and Nora's younger sister
- **Public goal:** Perform her duties and remain useful to the household
- **Private goal:** Win Ben's love and replace Nora as his wife
- **Secret:** She is having an affair with Ben
- **Possible secret:** She suspects that Ben is not Mia's biological father
- **Fear:** Being abandoned, ignored, or trapped in her low social position
- **Misbelief:** She believes taking Ben away from Nora will prove that she is more deserving of love
- **Personality:** Distant, observant, resentful, and emotionally guarded
- **Relationship with Ben:** Romantic and obsessive, though Ben may not be as committed as she believes
- **Relationship with Nora:** Loves her as a sister but resents her marriage, security, and closeness to Ben
- **Relationship with Mia:** Envious of the attention Mia receives from Ben and Nora
- **Arc:** From blaming Nora and Mia for her unhappiness to recognizing Ben's manipulation and taking responsibility for her own choices

## Miss Mia

- **Public role:** The young heiress and presumed daughter of Ben and Nora
- **Public goal:** Behave properly and become the daughter her parents expect her to be
- **Private goal:** Gain genuine affection and reassurance from her parents
- **Secret:** She feels unwanted and suspects that her family is hiding something from her
- **Secret she does not know:** The Owner is her biological father, while Ben is her uncle
- **Fear:** Losing her family or discovering that her identity is based on a lie
- **Misbelief:** She believes that if she is perfect enough, her parents will finally love her openly
- **Personality:** Haughty, sensitive, impulsive, and desperate for reassurance
- **Relationship with Ben:** Sees him as her father and constantly seeks his approval
- **Relationship with Nora:** Loves her mother but resents being protected and treated like a child
- **Relationship with the Owner:** Feels strangely connected to him without understanding why
- **Arc:** From seeking love through jealousy and emotional manipulation to defining herself independently of her family's secrets

## Nurse Nora

- **Public role:** Household nurse, Ben's wife, and Mia's mother
- **Public goal:** Keep the family safe and prevent the household from falling apart
- **Private goal:** Protect Mia from the truth while secretly hoping someone will acknowledge the harm done to her
- **Secret:** The Owner took advantage of her, and she has concealed the circumstances surrounding Mia's birth
- **Possible secret:** She suspects that Mia is the Owner's daughter but has chosen not to reveal it
- **Fear:** Losing Mia, being abandoned by Ben, or exposing the family to scandal
- **Misbelief:** She believes silently enduring pain is the same as protecting the people she loves
- **Personality:** Caring, patient, protective, and emotionally repressed
- **Relationship with Ben:** Loves him but feels increasingly controlled, neglected, and betrayed by him
- **Relationship with Madelyn:** Loves her as a sister but may sense that Madelyn is hiding something
- **Relationship with Mia:** Deeply protective, sometimes to the point of being overbearing
- **Relationship with the Owner:** Hates and fears him, but feels trapped by his power and the secret he left behind
- **Arc:** From suppressing the truth to protect her family to confronting the people who harmed her and choosing honesty

## The Owner

- **Public role:** Wealthy manor owner and head of the family
- **Public goal:** Ensure that his estate and will are handled according to his wishes
- **Private goal:** Maintain control over his family even after death and force his secrets into the open
- **Secret:** He is Mia's biological father
- **Possible secret:** He deliberately designed the will to manipulate the household after his death
- **Fear:** Losing control over his family, reputation, and legacy
- **Misbelief:** He believes wealth and intelligence give him the right to control other people's lives
- **Personality:** Charismatic, calculating, possessive, and emotionally cruel
- **Relationship with Ben:** Treats his younger brother as both a rival and an inferior
- **Relationship with Nora:** Exploited her and uses Mia's parentage to maintain power over her
- **Relationship with Mia:** Claims to care about her but primarily views her as part of his legacy
- **Arc:** His influence is gradually exposed, revealing that the mystery is partly his final attempt to control the family

Design rule: a character's _public goal_ should be what they claim to want, while their _private goal_ is what actually drives their choices. The detective can uncover the private goals gradually through conversations, evidence, and different time loops.
